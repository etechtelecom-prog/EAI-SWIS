# 10. Administrator Manual

## Responsibilities

- Manage organizations, users, roles, and permissions.
- Maintain categories, operational areas, and map layers.
- Configure API endpoints and storage.
- Review logs and failed synchronization.
- Perform backups and restoration tests.
- Coordinate releases and incident response.
- Enforce retention and access policies.

## Access control model

Recommended roles:
- platform administrator;
- organization administrator;
- executive viewer;
- dispatcher/operations manager;
- field officer;
- analyst;
- auditor/read-only user.

Permissions should be assigned by least privilege and scoped by organization and area.
